  <files></files>

  <what-built>
    A single-shot non-SCC energy path for f-containing systems (plan 04-01), a spin-polarization
    guard (04-01), the PME dtype repair (04-02), shell-resolved f data validation with a
    silent-zero guard on the shell-resolved Coulomb builder (04-03), and an Eu-N energy scan
    that locates the binding minimum and asserts it against a 2.655 Å ±20% band (04-04).

    At plan time the scan produced a single clean well with its minimum at 2.40 Å, comfortably
    inside the band. The automated gate checks that the minimum is interior, inside the band,
    unique on the grid, and that energies decrease into it and increase out of it.
  </what-built>

  <how-to-verify>
    1. Run `uv run pytest tests/test_eu_n_scan.py -q -s` so the scan diagnostics print, or read
       the curve table recorded in `04-04-SUMMARY.md`.

    2. Judge the curve shape. It must be a single well: energy falling steeply from the short
       end, one turning point, then a gentle rise toward the long end. If the curve is
       monotonic, has multiple wells, or is flat within numerical noise across the band, the
       band assertion is meaningless regardless of whether it passed. Report that.

    3. Judge the minimum's location against the band [2.124 Å, 3.186 Å], applying D-24's
       asymmetry:
       - Inside the band → approve.
       - **Short** of 2.124 Å → this is plausibly correct isolated-diatomic physics, not an f
         bug. The 2.655 Å target is a mean over dative Eu-N bonds in crowded 8-9 coordinate
         Eu-Bp complexes; a gas-phase diatomic would plausibly be shorter. Compare against bulk
         rock-salt EuN at roughly 2.45-2.5 Å and against the curve shape, then decide whether
         to widen the band (a new decision to record in CONTEXT.md) or to investigate. Do not
         conclude the f code is broken from a short minimum alone.
       - **Long** of 3.186 Å, or no interior minimum → genuine red flag. Investigate before
         approving.

    4. Confirm you accept the four limitations `04-VALIDATION.md` records as unsampled by this
       phase: the ±20% band absorbs a 15% f-block error; single-shot is not SCF, so
       charge-self-consistency bugs cannot surface; Eu 4f7 is treated closed-shell; and the
       shell-resolved plumbing is validated but never consumed.
  </how-to-verify>

  <verify>
    <human-check>A person has read the curve, judged its shape, applied D-24's asymmetric rule to the minimum's location, and recorded a verdict of approve / investigate / widen-band.</human-check>
  </verify>

  <acceptance_criteria>
    - `04-05-SUMMARY.md` records the observed minimum separation, the observed curve shape verdict (single well / monotonic / multi-well / flat), and one of the three dispositions: approved, investigate, or widen-band-with-new-decision.

    - If the disposition is investigate, the specific observation that triggered it is recorded verbatim.
    - The summary records explicit acknowledgement of the four unsampled limitations.
  </acceptance_criteria>
