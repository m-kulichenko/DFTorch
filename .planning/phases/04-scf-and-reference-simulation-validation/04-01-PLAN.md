**Why no Coulomb term (load-bearing — do not "improve" this).** Two candidate single-shot
definitions were scanned over separation:

| Definition | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Band + repulsion, no charge-fluctuation term (non-SCC DFTB1) | Clean binding well, minimum at 2.40 Å |
| Band + repulsion + Coulomb built from the first-iterate Mulliken charges | No interior well; energy rises monotonically past 2.0 Å |

The second is not "single-shot energy", it is one broken SCF step: the first-iterate Mulliken
charges are `q_Eu ≈ -2.7` and drift to `-2.99` at 4.0 Å, i.e. the atoms are not even neutral
at infinite separation. Feeding those into the Coulomb energy destroys the binding curve and
would make the SIM-05 gate in plan 04 unsatisfiable. D-11's wording — "build H0/S,
diagonalize once, report band/total energy" — is the first definition. Keep `structure.q` as
a diagnostic; keep it out of the energy.

## One flagged boundary — surfaced, not dropped

The seven f angular blocks (s-f, f-s, p-f, f-p, d-f, f-d, f-f) of the **shell-resolved Coulomb
matrix** are not implemented by this plan set. They are refused with a named exception rather
than approximated or silently zeroed. The blocking reason is a dependency conflict, not
difficulty: the shell-resolved matrix is `(n_shells, n_shells)` while `energy()` and `SCFx`
consume `(Nats, Nats)` with per-atom charges, so nothing in this phase could consume or
validate those values — shell-resolved charges through SCF are exactly what D-11 defers. See
`04-03-PLAN.md` § "Scope boundary" for the full reasoning.
